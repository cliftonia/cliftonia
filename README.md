### Selected projects

| Project | What it is |
|---|---|
| [**Hearth**](https://github.com/cliftonia/Hearth) | A private chat app for your own Ollama server, on iPhone, iPad and Mac, with an [Android sibling](https://github.com/cliftonia/HearthAndroid). Streamed replies, web search through your own SearXNG, hands-free voice, and pictures made on your own ComfyUI. SwiftUI with zero dependencies; Kotlin and Jetpack Compose on Android. Nothing leaves your own hardware. |
| [**YTV**](https://github.com/cliftonia/ytv) | A retro cable-TV dial for Android TV: around 130 channels of YouTube clips and live news, each playing at the offset the wall clock implies. No server: the lineup is rebuilt nightly by GitHub Actions and YouTube is resolved on the device, with a daily canary that tells a broken extractor apart from a blocked runner. 1,300+ commits, 30 releases. |
| [**Stepkin**](https://github.com/cliftonia/Stepkin) | A virtual pet for Apple Watch, inspired by late-'90s LCD pets. Swift 6, zero dependencies, a pure `nonisolated` simulation engine, HealthKit steps drive evolution and WeatherKit drives the scene. Pixel-exact LCD snapshot tests in CI. |
| [**Subspace**](https://github.com/cliftonia/Subspace) | An iOS messaging app in SwiftUI with Clean Architecture, WebSockets, Sign in with Apple and its own [Go backend](https://github.com/cliftonia/LCARS-BE-GO). Runs fully offline against an on-device mock of the API. |
| [**Deep Orbit**](https://github.com/cliftonia/DeepOrbit) | A space strategy game for iPhone in SpriteKit: procedural galaxy, AI factions, fleets and save/load. |
